How the quiz works

In Letters mode a Hangul letter appears and you choose its roman sound from four options. All the consonants, double consonants, vowels and compound vowels from our Hangul lessons are included. In Words mode a Korean word appears and you choose its English meaning; the words come from our word lists. After each answer the correct one turns green and the full reading is shown. The Listen button reads the letter or word if your device has a Korean voice. Your score lasts only while the page is open, and nothing is saved or sent.

How to practise well

  1. Say the answer aloud before you look at the options. Recalling it yourself builds memory far more strongly than recognising it.
  2. Aim for a streak. Twenty correct letters in a row means you know the alphabet. Fewer means one more round.
  3. Note what you miss. Most learners mix up the same pairs: ㅓ and ㅗ, ㅜ and ㅡ, ㅐ and ㅔ, ㅈ and ㅊ. Write your problem pair side by side ten times.
  4. Five minutes, often. A short round while waiting for a bus does more than one long session a week.

Why a letter may have two right-looking answers

Some compound vowels sound nearly the same in modern speech, for example ㅙ (wae), ㅚ (oe) and ㅞ (we). The quiz asks for the official roman spelling of each letter, which keeps them apart. Learning the spellings separately will help you write correctly later.

After the quiz

When letters feel easy, read whole phrases. Open greetings, cover the roman line with your hand and read the Hangul aloud. That is the real test, and passing it is a fine feeling.
