Food and eating out

16 phrases in Korean, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Korean voice.

Food is at the centre of Korean life and of nearly every drama, so these phrases come up constantly. Ordering is simple: name the dish and add 주세요 (juseyo). 김밥 주세요: "gimbap, please."

Two set phrases frame every meal. Before eating, Koreans say 잘 먹겠습니다 (jal meokgetseumnida), "I will eat well", as thanks to whoever cooked or is paying. After eating they say 잘 먹었습니다 (jal meogeotseumnida), "I ate well". You will hear both in every family scene on screen.

For Indian vegetarians, a word of practical caution: much Korean food contains meat, fish sauce or seafood stock even when it looks like vegetables, and kimchi is often made with fish sauce. The phrases below let you say that you are vegetarian and ask for meat to be left out, but in Korea it is wise to ask about the stock and sauces too. Spice lovers will feel at home; 매워요 (maewoyo), "it is spicy", is said with pride.

At the table

  • In many Korean restaurants water and small side dishes (반찬, banchan) come without being ordered.
  • Wait for the eldest person to pick up the spoon before you start; it is the Korean form of the respect we show elders at an Indian table.
  • 맛있어요 said to the cook or the host is the best compliment you can give. Add 진짜 (jinjja, "really") in front for extra warmth.
