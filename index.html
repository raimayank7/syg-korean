    Who this site is for

    It is for the Indian fan who has watched a hundred episodes with subtitles and wants to understand a little without them; for the reader of translated Korean web novels who wonders what oppa and sunbae really mean; and for the student thinking about Korean as a third language. You need no earlier knowledge. If you can read this page, you can learn the Korean alphabet in a week.

    Why Hindi helps

    Korean and Hindi are unrelated, yet they build sentences in the same way: the verb comes last, small markers follow the noun as में, से, को do, and respect is shown through word endings as with तू, तुम, आप. Most courses are written for English speakers and treat all this as strange and hard. For you it is home ground. Read Korean for Hindi speakers to see how far the likeness goes, and where it stops.

    Common questions

    Can I really learn Hangul in a week?

    Yes. Hangul has 24 basic letters with regular shapes, and spelling follows sound closely. With 20 to 30 minutes a day, most learners can sound out Korean words slowly within a week. Understanding the words takes longer, of course.

    Is Korean hard for Hindi speakers?

    The grammar is easier for Hindi speakers than for English speakers, because word order, postpositions and levels of respect work in a similar way. The vocabulary is almost entirely new, and that is where the real effort goes.

    What does oppa mean?

    It is what a female calls an older brother or an older male she is close to. A male calls the same person hyeong. It does not mean boyfriend, though a girlfriend may call her older boyfriend oppa.

    Why does the site write "eo" and "eu"?

    These are the official roman spellings of two Korean vowels. "Eo" is one sound, like the o in "office". "Eu" is like "u" said with flat, unrounded lips. The romanization page explains the whole system.

    Does the site have audio?

    It stores no audio files. The Listen button asks your own phone or computer to read the Korean text aloud, which works if the device has a Korean voice installed.
