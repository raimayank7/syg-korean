Food words

21 words in Korean, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Korean voice.

The first word on any Korean food list has to be 밥 (bap). It means cooked rice, and it also means "a meal", because for Koreans, as for much of India, a meal without rice is hardly a meal. The friendly greeting 밥 먹었어요? (bap meogeosseoyo?), "Have you eaten?", is a way of asking after someone, just like "खाना खाया?"

Many dish names are simple compounds that you can read once you know a few pieces. 김밥 (gimbap) is 김 seaweed + 밥 rice. 비빔밥 (bibimbap) is "mixed rice". 볶음밥 (bokkeumbap) is "fried rice". Words borrowed from English are written in Hangul by sound: 커피 (keopi) coffee, 주스 (juseu) juice, 아이스크림 (aiseukeurim) ice cream. Reading these aloud is excellent Hangul practice.

Tea is 차 (cha), the same word as our चाय, both going back to the same Chinese root.

Notes

  • "사과" means both "apple" and "apology", which Korean writers enjoy using for wordplay.
  • You will see "kimchi" and "gimchi": the first is the long-established English spelling, the second follows the official romanisation of 김치.
  • To order any of these, add 주세요. To say you like one, add 좋아해요: 김밥 좋아해요, "I like gimbap".
