How to use the phrase pages

Each page begins with a short explanation of the language point that matters most for that situation, then lists the phrases as cards. On every card the Hangul comes first, because that is the real thing; the roman line under it is only a help for your first weeks. The Hindi line gives the natural meaning, and the English line follows. Tap Listen to hear the phrase if your phone or computer has a Korean voice, and Copy to paste it into a chat or your notes.

All phrases are in the polite -요 style or the formal -습니다 style unless a card says "casual". These are safe with anyone. Why that matters is explained in politeness levels.

Which page first?

Begin with greetings and thank you, sorry, yes and no. Fans of dramas and web novels usually want the words they keep hearing next, and that page will explain oppa, sunbae and daebak once and for all. If you study with a teacher or a language partner, the classroom phrases let you ask your questions in Korean.

Learn phrases as wholes

Do not take a phrase apart on the first day. Say it aloud ten times as one piece, like the line of a song. Only when it comes easily, look at how it is built. Then change one word to make a new sentence: 물 주세요 (water, please) becomes 차 주세요 (tea, please). That small step, from a learnt phrase to a made one, is where speaking begins.
