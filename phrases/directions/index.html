Directions and travel

15 phrases in Korean, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Korean voice.

The question word for places is 어디 (eodi), "where", and the travel sentence everyone needs is "... 어디예요?": where is ...? Put any place in front of it. 화장실이 어디예요? "Where is the toilet?" is, by common agreement, the most useful sentence in any language.

Direction words are few: 오른쪽 (oreunjjok) right, 왼쪽 (oenjjok) left, 직진 (jikjin) straight ahead, 여기 (yeogi) here, 저기 (jeogi) over there. In a taxi, name the place and add 가 주세요, "please go", and say 여기서 세워 주세요, "please stop here", when you arrive.

The particles do in Korean what postpositions do in Hindi. 에 (e) is "to" or "at", as in स्कूल को; 에서 (eseo) is "from" or "at (doing something)"; 까지 (kkaji) is "up to", exactly like तक: 공항까지, हवाई अड्डे तक.

  • 화장실이 어디예요?

    hwajangsiri eodiyeyo?

    शौचालय कहाँ है?

    Where is the toilet?

  • 지하철역이 어디예요?

    jihacheollyeogi eodiyeyo?

    मेट्रो स्टेशन कहाँ है?

    Where is the subway station?

  • 여기가 어디예요?

    yeogiga eodiyeyo?

    यह कौन-सी जगह है?

    Where am I? What place is this?

  • 길을 잃었어요

    gireul ireosseoyo

    मैं रास्ता भटक गया हूँ

    I am lost

  • 오른쪽으로 가세요

    oreunjjogeuro gaseyo

    दाईं ओर जाइए

    Go to the right

  • 왼쪽으로 가세요

    oenjjogeuro gaseyo

    बाईं ओर जाइए

    Go to the left

  • 직진하세요

    jikjinhaseyo

    सीधे जाइए

    Go straight

  • 멀어요?

    meoreoyo?

    क्या दूर है?

    Is it far?

  • 가까워요

    gakkawoyo

    पास ही है

    It is near

  • 공항까지 가 주세요

    gonghangkkaji ga juseyo

    हवाई अड्डे तक चलिए

    Please take me to the airport

  • 여기서 세워 주세요

    yeogiseo sewo juseyo

    यहीं रोक दीजिए

    Please stop here

  • 표 한 장 주세요

    pyo han jang juseyo

    एक टिकट दीजिए

    One ticket, please

  • 몇 시에 출발해요?

    myeot sie chulbalhaeyo?

    कितने बजे चलेगी?

    What time does it leave?

  • 이 버스 서울역에 가요?

    i beoseu seoullyeoge gayo?

    क्या यह बस सियोल स्टेशन जाती है?

    Does this bus go to Seoul Station?

  • 지도를 보여 주세요

    jidoreul boyeo juseyo

    कृपया नक़्शा दिखाइए

    Please show me on the map

Place words to combine

역 (yeok) station, 공항 (gonghang) airport, 호텔 (hotel) hotel, 병원 (byeongwon) hospital, 은행 (eunhaeng) bank, 편의점 (pyeonuijeom) convenience store, 식당 (sikdang) restaurant, 대사관 (daesagwan) embassy. Put any of them before 어디예요? to ask where it is.
