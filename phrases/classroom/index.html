Learning and classroom phrases

15 phrases in Korean, each in the script with a roman reading, the Hindi meaning and the English meaning. Tap Listen to hear it, where your device has a Korean voice.

The most valuable Korean you can learn early is the Korean for learning Korean. With these phrases you can keep a conversation going in the language even when you do not understand, instead of falling back on English. Teachers and language partners love a student who asks "what does this mean?" in Korean.

Three question frames carry the page. "... 뭐예요?" asks what something is. "... 한국어로 뭐예요?" asks how to say something in Korean; 로 here means "in" or "by means of", like में in "हिंदी में क्या कहते हैं?". And "... 무슨 뜻이에요?" asks what a word means.

The classroom is also where you meet the formal command endings teachers use, -세요 (seyo): 읽으세요 "please read", 쓰세요 "please write", 따라 하세요 "please repeat after me".
